Syngenta announced it will cease the sale of paraquat in Australia due to new regulations linking the herbicide to Parkinson's disease. This decision follows restrictions imposed by the Australian Pesticides and Veterinary Medicines Authority in June. This decision is significant as paraquat has been banned in over 70 countries due to its health risks, particularly its association with Parkinson's disease. The move reflects growing regulatory scrutiny on harmful chemicals in agriculture. Paraquat is a highly toxic herbicide that has been linked to serious health issues, including Parkinson's disease. The new regulations have rendered its sale commercially unviable for Syngenta in Australia.

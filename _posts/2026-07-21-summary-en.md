It also reflects broader global health trends that could affect healthcare systems and public health policies. The study indicates that the rise in antibiotic resistance is linked to factors such as the misuse and overuse of antibiotics in both healthcare and agriculture. Additionally, children with compromised immune systems, like those on immunosuppressive medications, are particularly vulnerable.

**Background**: Antibiotic resistance occurs when bacteria evolve to resist the effects of medications designed to kill them, making infections harder to treat. Children, especially those with underlying health conditions, are at a higher risk of severe infections due to this resistance.

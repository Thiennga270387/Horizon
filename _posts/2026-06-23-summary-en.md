## [Drug that delays onset of type 1 diabetes approved in England and Wales](https://www.theguardian.com/society/2026/jun/23/drug-that-delays-onset-of-type-1-diabetes-approved-in-england-and-wales) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Teplizumab has been approved for use on the NHS in England and Wales, marking a significant advancement in diabetes treatment. This drug can postpone the early stages of type 1 diabetes for up to three years. This approval is significant as it represents the first drug capable of delaying the onset of type 1 diabetes, potentially impacting millions of individuals worldwide. It could change the management of diabetes and improve the quality of life for those at risk. Teplizumab is a CD3-directed monoclonal antibody that modifies T-cell immune behavior to reduce cytotoxicity. Its approval follows a rigorous evaluation process by the NHS to ensure its safety and effectiveness.

rss · The Guardian World · Jun 23, 05:00

**Background**: Type 1 diabetes is an autoimmune condition that leads to the destruction of insulin-producing beta cells in the pancreas, resulting in insulin deficiency. Teplizumab works by binding to CD3 receptors on T cells, which helps to modulate the immune response and delay the progression of the disease.

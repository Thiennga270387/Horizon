## [Twins' Rare Growth Disorder May Help Prevent Cancer](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c8jnlmek8reo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Researchers have discovered that individuals with Laron syndrome exhibit a significantly lower incidence of cancer compared to the general population. This finding suggests a potential link between the disorder and cancer prevention. This discovery could reshape our understanding of cancer prevention strategies, potentially leading to new therapeutic approaches. It highlights the importance of genetic factors in cancer risk and opens avenues for further research. Laron syndrome is characterized by growth hormone insensitivity and is extremely rare, with only about 250 known cases worldwide. Individuals with this syndrome are unresponsive to growth hormone therapy, which makes the study of their cancer incidence particularly valuable.

rss · BBC World News · Jul 17, 23:24

**Background**: Laron syndrome is an autosomal recessive disorder caused by mutations in the growth hormone receptor, leading to a lack of insulin-like growth factor 1 (IGF-1). Individuals with this condition typically present with short stature and other health issues, but recent studies suggest they may also have a lower risk of developing cancer and type II diabetes.
